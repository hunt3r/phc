PH&C SERVICES — CONTENT DRAFT
================================

This document contains roughly one page of starting content for each of PH&C's

================================================================================
PRE-CONSTRUCTION SERVICES
================================================================================

A successful project begins long before ground is broken. It requires meticulous
planning and the careful coordination of many disciplines, from architects and
engineers to municipal authorities and utility providers. PH&C brings the
expertise and the broad, experienced perspective needed to manage the design,
entitlement, constructability, and cost processes that set a project up to
succeed. We treat pre-construction not as paperwork to get through, but as the
single most important phase for protecting your budget, your schedule, and your
vision.

PH&C delivers value during pre-construction by focusing on several key areas.
First, we help clients assemble the right team for their specific project,
drawing on a large network of experienced consultants across every discipline.
The right architect, civil engineer, geotechnical firm, and specialty consultant
can be the difference between a project that flows and one that stalls. Next, by
proactively guiding the design and entitlement process against critical-path
milestones, we reduce delays and save valuable time and money. We do not wait for
problems to surface; we anticipate approvals, permits, and long-lead decisions and
keep every party moving in the same direction.

At the same time, we gather cost feedback on critical design decisions and fold
value-engineering suggestions into the design before procurement begins. By
managing cost and expectations before construction starts, we eliminate the
expensive surprises and change orders that so often derail projects once they are
underway. The result is a design that is not only beautiful and functional, but
buildable, permittable, and aligned with your financial goals.

PH&C guides clients through the demanding process of design and land development,
including:

Project Planning & Coordination
  - Initial Program Development
  - Schematic Design and Design Development Documents
  - Special Construction Design Coordination
  - Project Schedule
  - Proforma Analysis / Hard & Soft Cost Budgeting
  - Detailed Project Estimating and Value Engineering
  - Cash Flow Analysis
  - Land Development & Zoning Approvals
  - Highway Permitting
  - NPDES Permitting
  - Other Environmental: Stream Crossing / Sewer Modules
  - Utility Service Coordination: Water / Sewer / Gas / Phone / Electric / Cable
  - Building / Construction Permits
  - Pre-Construction Meetings

Project Contractor Procurement
  - RFP Development
  - Contractor Bidding / Clarification / Selection
  - Contractor Negotiations / Contract Development

By the time your project is ready to break ground, every decision has been vetted,
every cost has been tested, and every approval is in hand. That is the PH&C
pre-construction difference.


================================================================================
CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT SERVICES
================================================================================

Once a project moves into construction, PH&C manages every activity to ensure a
successful completion. Construction begins with a detailed plan, aggressively
executed, and adapts to changes as they inevitably arise. No project unfolds
exactly as drawn; the difference is whether those changes are managed proactively
or reacted to after they have already cost time and money. Our experienced staff
works alongside contractors to anticipate issues and solve problems before they
occur, keeping the project moving and the budget intact.

PH&C serves as the link between clients and contractors, giving our clients the
expertise to engage contractors on equal footing. Construction is a specialized
world with its own language, standards, and pressures. We understand the
intricacies of complex design and construction issues and develop innovative
solutions that work for both owner and contractor, avoiding the excess cost and
schedule delays that come from miscommunication or adversarial relationships. Our
clients gain a knowledgeable advocate at the table who protects their interests
while keeping the project collaborative and productive.

Throughout construction, we maintain rigorous oversight of schedule, budget, and
quality. We document conditions, coordinate the many agencies and vendors involved,
and manage the financial mechanics of the project so that billing, inspections, and
approvals move without friction. When issues arise, we resolve them quickly and
decisively, always with an eye on the owner's ultimate goals.

Detailed construction services include:

  - Construction Coordination, Supervision, and Documentation
  - Requests for Information / Design Issue Recommendation / Resolution
  - Government Agency Coordination
  - Utility Coordination
  - Testing Agency Coordination
  - Tenant Delivery / Construction Coordination
  - Project Schedule Management
  - Contract Management / Budget Management
  - Bank Billing & Inspection Coordination / Government Agency
  - Escrow & Letter of Credit Reductions

From the first mobilization to final closeout, PH&C keeps your project on schedule,
on budget, and built to the standard you expect.

OWNER'S REPRESENTATION / PROJECT MANAGEMENT OVERSIGHT
================================================================================

PH&C's services include complete program management, or owner's representative,
services. Acting as the owner's agent and single point of control, PH&C oversees,
manages, and administers the entire project, or any portion you choose to delegate.
For owners without an in-house construction team, or for those whose teams are
already stretched, we become the trusted authority who ensures the project is
delivered exactly as intended.

Our program management consulting functions as if PH&C were the real estate and
construction department within your firm. In that capacity, we report, coordinate,
oversee, and work within the goals and parameters you define. You set the vision,
the budget, and the priorities; we handle the day-to-day management, decision-making,
and coordination required to bring them to life. You retain full visibility and
control without having to manage the countless details yourself.

This model gives you the development, design, and construction expertise you need to
achieve your goals, without the overhead costs of in-house personnel. You gain a
seasoned professional who has managed projects of every type and scale, who knows
where risks hide, and who protects your interests at every turn. Whether you need
oversight of a single project or ongoing management of a broader program, PH&C
delivers the accountability, transparency, and results that owners rely on.

DRONE TECHNOLOGIES
================================================================================

PH&C employs drone aircraft technology at every step of the process to provide
insight and a different perspective on a project's overview and possibilities. From
the earliest site evaluation through final closeout, aerial imagery reveals context,
scale, and detail that cannot be captured from the ground. It helps owners, designers,
and stakeholders see the whole picture and make better-informed decisions at every
phase.

Documenting site conditions with high-resolution imagery from beginning to end is an
invaluable tool across a wide range of scenarios. During pre-construction, aerial
surveys inform planning, feasibility, and design. During construction, regular flights
create a visual record of progress, support documentation and reporting, and help
verify that work is proceeding as planned. That same record is invaluable for
resolving questions, supporting inspections and billing, and demonstrating progress to
lenders, agencies, and stakeholders.

The result is greater transparency, better communication, and a comprehensive visual
history of your project from the ground up. It is one more way PH&C brings modern tools
and a fresh perspective to deliver clarity and confidence at every stage.
