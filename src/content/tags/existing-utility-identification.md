The utilities that cause change orders are often the ones already in the ground. PH&C identifies and assesses them before the estimate is treated as final. An old plan only suggests what is buried. An assessment confirms the line, the size, and the owner.

## What PH&C handles

- Gathering utility company records, one-call information, and survey data.
- Locating water, sewer, storm, gas, electric, communications, and private lines on and near the site.
- Identifying conflicts with the proposed building, parking, walls, and stormwater facilities.
- Checking capacity, depth, and condition of existing service.
- Requesting capacity or will-serve letters from the providers when loads are significant.
- Documenting easements that affect how lines can be relocated.
- Carrying relocation and extension costs into the budget.

## What you get

- A site plan that shows existing utilities and the conflicts they create.
- Early notice of lines that must be moved, protected, or upgraded.
- Capacity information to support your use and your lease.
- Fewer extra-trench change orders and fewer surprises during demolition.
