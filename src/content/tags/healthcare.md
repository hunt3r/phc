Healthcare providers need facilities that match demanding and changing requirements. PH&C works closely with providers to plan and construct new facilities, or renovate existing ones, so they serve both the practice and its tenants.

## What PH&C handles

- Program development: exam rooms, procedure rooms, patient flow, and staff areas.
- Site selection support, feasibility, and land development approvals.
- Renovations of existing buildings, including interior demolition and hazardous material surveys.
- Coordination of specialty systems, such as imaging equipment, medical gas, and technology.
- Tenant improvement and landlord work for medical office buildings.
- Construction management of new buildings, additions, and fit-outs.
- Phasing work in occupied buildings so patient care continues.
- Coordination of licensing, inspections, and certificates of occupancy.

## What you get

- A construction manager who works with your clinical and administrative staff.
- Budgets and schedules that match your opening and licensing dates.
- Designs coordinated with equipment vendors and technology consultants.
- Construction logistics that respect patient and staff access.
