On a development, the planting plan is a condition of approval as much as a design choice. PH&C bids and manages landscaping and irrigation so the work meets the approved plan and the owner's budget, and so it is installed when the plants have the best chance of survival.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing the approved planting plan and the conditions the municipality attached to it.
- Coordinating the planting designer, the civil engineer, and the contractors who share the site.
- Bidding planting, irrigation, lawn, and mulch as separate items or as part of the site contract.
- Scheduling installation around paving, utilities, and the planting season.
- Observing plant quality, sizing, and placement against the drawings.
- Documenting warranty periods and replacement requirements.
- Coordinating inspections and the release of any security tied to planting.

## What you get

- A planting budget that is separate from the building budget and visible to the owner.
- A schedule that avoids installing plants in the wrong season.
- Documentation for the municipality, the lender, and the tenant.
- A finished site that matches the approved plan.
