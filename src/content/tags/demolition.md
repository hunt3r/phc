Demolition is the first construction work on many redevelopment sites, and its cost depends on what is found inside and underneath the building. PH&C manages demolition for the owner, from the first survey through the cleared, graded site.

## What PH&C handles

- Surveying the building and site, including hazardous material surveys and underground conditions.
- Defining the scope: structures, slabs, foundations, pavement, tanks, and site features.
- Identifying utilities that must be shut off, capped, or relocated before work starts.
- Preparing bid documents and comparing contractor proposals.
- Coordinating abatement, notifications, and permits with the governing agencies.
- Managing neighbors and occupied buildings that remain in use during the work.
- Observing salvage, recycling, hauling, and disposal records.
- Confirming grading, backfill, and erosion controls at the end of the work.

## What you get

- A priced scope that reflects what the survey found.
- Demolition sequenced with abatement, remediation, and the permits that precede new construction.
- A documented record of waste handling and site conditions.
- A cleared site ready for the next phase of work.
