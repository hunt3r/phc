Grant funding comes with rules about eligible costs, procurement, reporting, and deadlines. PH&C manages grant-funded construction for the owner so the project is built and documented in a way the funding agency can accept.

## What PH&C handles

- Reading the grant agreement and listing the requirements that affect construction.
- Separating eligible and ineligible costs in the budget.
- Structuring bids and contracts to follow the program's procurement rules.
- Aligning the project schedule with grant milestones and expiration dates.
- Preparing payment requests and supporting documentation.
- Keeping records for audits, including certified payroll and compliance forms where the program requires them.
- Coordinating with the grant administrator, the municipality, and the design team.
- Reporting progress in the format the program expects.

## What you get

- A budget that shows what the grant covers and what the owner pays.
- Fewer delayed reimbursements caused by missing documentation.
- A project file organized for the funding agency's review.
- Early notice when a change could affect eligibility.
