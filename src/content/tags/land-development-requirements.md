Before an owner prices a site, someone has to read the ordinance. PH&C does that review. The product is a short memo covering the zoning district, the permitted uses, the bulk limits, and the approval path, with every item that needs relief marked.

## What PH&C handles

- Confirming if the intended use is permitted by right, by conditional use, or by special exception.
- Checking density, building height, setbacks, impervious coverage, and parking ratios against your concept.
- Identifying buffers, street trees, planting requirements, and stormwater rules that reduce usable area.
- Determining whether subdivision, land development, a variance, or a rezoning is required.
- Listing the studies staff will expect on the first submission, such as traffic and stormwater.
- Noting outside agencies whose approval runs alongside the municipality's.
- Estimating the approval timeline.

## What you get

- A clear view of what can be built and what cannot.
- A side-by-side comparison when you are choosing between two parcels.
- An early estimate of approval time and risk.
- A list of requirements that carry cost, ready for your budget.

## How it connects

The review is part of [site analysis and feasibility](/services/site-analysis-feasibility).
