Grocery stores combine a retailer's prototype with a local site, and the two have to agree. PH&C manages the development and construction of grocery stores and supermarkets across the region, working with retailers such as Lidl, ShopRite, and ALDI.

Our experience spans ground-up construction, existing shell conversions to grocery prototypes, and grocery-anchored shopping center developments.

## What PH&C handles

- Site selection support, feasibility, and land development approvals.
- Highway access and traffic improvements for delivery trucks and customer traffic.
- Stormwater, utility, and environmental permits.
- Reviewing the retailer's prototype and criteria against the site and the budget.
- Delivering pad sites and shells that meet the retailer's requirements.
- Construction management of the store, including refrigeration, dock, and sales floor work.
- Coordinating the retailer's operations team for turnover and opening.
- Adapting an existing building to a current prototype.

## What you get

- A single manager who understands both the retailer's standards and the municipality's.
- A schedule built around opening dates that retailers announce in advance.
- Site and building costs identified early.
- A coordinated handoff that includes punch list, inspections, and closeout documents.
