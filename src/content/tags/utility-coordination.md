Once a project is under construction, utility work depends on crews and inspectors who do not report to your contractor. PH&C coordinates those companies so service arrives in the order the schedule requires.

## What PH&C handles

- Confirming each provider's design is approved and the fees and deposits are paid.
- Sequencing utility work with site work, paving, and building completion.
- Scheduling provider crews, inspectors, and shutdowns.
- Coordinating cutovers on occupied sites at times that suit existing tenants.
- Confirming locations and clearances against the civil drawings.
- Tracking the inspection and acceptance of new mains and services.
- Coordinating meters, final connections, and energization dates.
- Keeping a log of each utility's status, contacts, and promised dates.

## What you get

- Service available when the building is ready for it.
- Fewer conflicts between utility crews and the contractor's work.
- Early notice when a provider's schedule affects the opening date.
- Documentation of utility acceptance for your lender, your tenants, and your records.

## How it connects

Applications and designs are managed earlier under [utility service coordination](/services/utility-service-coordination). Existing lines are identified under [existing utility identification](/services/existing-utility-identification). Both support [construction management](/services/construction-management-services).
