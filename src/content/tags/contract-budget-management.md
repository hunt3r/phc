The contract says what the contractor owes. The budget says what you can spend. PH&C administers both every month until the last retainage payment.

## What PH&C handles

- Setting up the project budget by line item, from the executed contracts and soft costs.
- Reviewing pay applications against work in place, stored materials, and the schedule of values.
- Collecting lien waivers and required documentation with each payment.
- Evaluating change-order requests for scope, price, and time.
- Tracking pending changes, which are costs even before they are approved.
- Managing allowances and unit-price work.
- Reporting contingency use and remaining balances.
- Closing out contracts, retainage, and final payments.

## What you get

- A monthly cost report showing original contract, approved changes, pending changes, amounts billed, and balance.
- Change orders priced and reviewed before the work proceeds.
- Pay applications you can approve with confidence.
- A budget that stays tied to your lender's and your partners' reporting.
