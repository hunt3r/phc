Redeveloping a site with a past industrial, fuel, or auto-service use often means cleanup before construction. PH&C manages that work for the owner so the cost is defined, the contractor is qualified, and the closure paperwork is in order.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing environmental reports and defining the cleanup scope.
- Coordinating the environmental consultant and the regulatory agencies.
- Preparing bid documents with unit prices for excavation, hauling, and disposal.
- Qualifying contractors and comparing proposals.
- Observing excavation, sampling, and confirmation testing.
- Tracking quantities, manifests, and disposal facilities.
- Coordinating tank removal, dewatering, and backfill with demolition and construction.
- Assembling the closure report and the records required by lenders, buyers, and regulators.

## What you get

- A cleanup budget with the unknowns identified and priced as allowances or unit rates.
- Remediation scheduled so it does not hold up permits or building construction.
- A transparent record of what was removed and where it went.
- Documentation to support financing and future sale.
