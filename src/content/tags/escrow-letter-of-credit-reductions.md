Development often requires escrows and letters of credit posted with municipalities and lenders, and recovering that capital as work is completed matters to the bottom line. PH&C manages escrow and letter-of-credit reductions - coordinating the inspections, certifications, and documentation required to release funds as improvements are completed and accepted.

We track the requirements, engage the agencies, and pursue reductions promptly so our clients' capital is not tied up longer than necessary. Actively managing these releases returns money to ownership as milestones are met.
