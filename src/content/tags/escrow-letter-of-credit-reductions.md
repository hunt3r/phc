Municipalities and lenders often hold cash or a letter of credit while a project is built. They release it in stages once their inspector confirms the work is complete. PH&C manages those reductions so your capital is not held longer than the agreement requires.

## What PH&C handles

- Reading the development agreement, ordinance, or loan terms to identify each release milestone.
- Keeping a log of every instrument, with the holder, the amount, and the conditions for reduction.
- Scheduling inspections when a milestone is complete.
- Obtaining engineer certifications, as-built drawings, test reports, and maintenance documentation.
- Submitting reduction requests with the attachments the holder expects.
- Following up with the municipality or lender until the release is processed.
- Coordinating the final release at the end of the maintenance period.

## What you get

- A clear record of what is posted, what has been released, and what is still held.
- Requests filed as soon as milestones are met.
- Fewer delays caused by missing documents.
- Capital returned earlier, which lowers the cost of carrying it.

## How it connects

Releases build on inspections coordinated through [government agency coordination](/services/government-agency-coordination) and on the records kept by [construction coordination](/services/construction-coordination-supervision).
