A request for information, or RFI, is how a contractor asks the design team what the drawings mean. PH&C runs that log for the owner so questions are answered quickly and the cost of each answer is known before work changes.

## What PH&C handles

- Receiving, numbering, and dating every RFI.
- Sending each question to the right designer and tracking the response time.
- Reviewing responses for cost and schedule effect before they are implemented.
- Separating design clarifications from changes in scope.
- Identifying patterns, such as many RFIs on one specification section, and raising them with the design team.
- Coordinating bulletins and revised drawings when an answer changes the documents.
- Linking RFIs to change orders and to the pay application record.
- Reporting open items to the owner.

## What you get

- A single log that shows each question, the response, and the outcome.
- Decisions made before the field has to guess.
- Fewer unplanned changes after work is in place.
- A record that supports your position if a claim arises.
