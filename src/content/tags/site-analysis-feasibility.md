Feasibility is the work you do before a deposit goes hard. PH&C leads site analysis, property assessment, and feasibility studies so you know what a parcel will allow, what it will cost, and how long approvals are likely to take. The goal is a decision: proceed, renegotiate, or pass.

## What PH&C handles

- Zoning and land development requirements, including permitted uses, density, setbacks, parking, and the approval path.
- Title commitment and survey review for easements, rights-of-way, encroachments, and deed restrictions.
- Environmental assessments and hazardous material surveys for properties with a prior use or an existing building.
- Geotechnical investigations that identify rock, groundwater, and soil conditions affecting foundations and earthwork.
- Existing utility identification and capacity checks for water, sewer, gas, electric, and communications.
- Highway access and the environmental permits the project is likely to need.
- Landlord and tenant lease construction requirements.
- A preliminary schedule and an opinion of probable cost tested against your pro forma.

## What you get

- A written summary of the risks, open questions, and costs found at each stage.
- Studies sequenced so the cheapest, highest-risk questions are answered first.
- A realistic timeline for approvals and construction.
- A budget that separates site work, building, and soft costs.
