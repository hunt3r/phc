A Phase I environmental site assessment is a records review and site inspection that asks whether a property's past use could have left contamination. A Phase II involves sampling when the Phase I identifies a concern. PH&C scopes both for the owner and reads the findings against your deal, your construction budget, and your schedule.

## What PH&C handles

- Selecting and managing the environmental consultant.
- Scoping a Phase I assessment to the lender's and purchaser's requirements.
- Reviewing the report for recognized environmental conditions.
- Planning Phase II sampling so boring and well locations match the future building, utilities, and excavation.
- Translating results into cleanup quantities, disposal costs, and permit requirements.
- Coordinating with the seller, lender, and regulators.
- Carrying findings into the estimate, the schedule, and the bid documents.

## What you get

- A clear statement of what is known and what remains uncertain.
- Cost and schedule effects of any contamination before your deposit becomes nonrefundable.
- A defined remediation scope contractors can price.
- Documentation to support lenders, buyers, and closure with regulators.
