A feasibility schedule is a test, not a promise. PH&C builds it before you purchase a property, to find out whether the date in your pro forma survives the approval process.

## What PH&C handles

- Laying out due diligence, design, land development and zoning, long-lead permits, procurement, and construction.
- Assigning durations that reflect how each agency and utility actually reviews projects.
- Identifying dependencies, such as a utility design that cannot start until the site plan is approved.
- Showing where float exists and where it does not.
- Comparing the result with lease dates, financing deadlines, and tenant commitments.
- Updating the schedule when a study or an agency response changes an assumption.

## What you get

- A realistic timeline before you spend design money.
- A list of the approvals most likely to affect your opening date.
- Information for decisions on financing, lease terms, and how aggressively to pursue the site.
- A starting point for the full [project schedule](/services/project-schedule) if you proceed.

## How it connects

The schedule is part of [site analysis and feasibility](/services/site-analysis-feasibility). It relies on inputs from [land development requirements](/services/land-development-requirements) and [environmental permitting requirements](/services/environmental-permitting-requirements).
