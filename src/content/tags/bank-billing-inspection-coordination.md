A lender funds a draw when someone it trusts confirms the work is in place. An invoice from the contractor does not do that alone. PH&C prepares the package and coordinates the inspection.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing each pay application against the schedule of values and the work observed on site.
- Documenting stored materials, with the evidence the lender requires.
- Collecting lien waivers, insurance certificates, and other draw documentation.
- Preparing the owner's draw request in the lender's format.
- Scheduling and attending lender and agency inspections.
- Reconciling inspection findings with the contractor's billing.
- Tracking retainage, holdbacks, and conditions for release.
- Coordinating draw requirements for grant and public funding programs.

## What you get

- Draws that are approved the first time more often than not.
- A documented link between each payment and the progress behind it.
- Fewer delays caused by missing waivers or lines that run ahead of the work.
- A clean record for your lender, your partners, and your auditors.
