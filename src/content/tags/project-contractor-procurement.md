PH&C buys the construction on your behalf. We write the bid documents, run the bidding, compare the proposals, and negotiate the contract. The result is a contractor who priced the work you think you are buying, on terms you have reviewed.

## What PH&C handles

- [RFP development](/services/rfp-development): scope, drawings, schedule, insurance, and contract terms in a package bidders can price the same way.
- Bidder lists built from contractors with relevant experience in your building type and region.
- Pre-bid meetings, site walks, and written addenda so every bidder works from the same information.
- [Bid leveling](/services/contractor-bidding-selection): base bids, alternates, unit prices, allowances, and exclusions compared side by side.
- [Contract negotiation](/services/contractor-negotiations-contracts): price, schedule, and terms agreed before work starts.
- Procurement records for public and grant-funded projects.

## What you get

- A bid tabulation that shows what each number includes and what it leaves out.
- A clear recommendation with the reasons behind it.
- An executed agreement that reflects the approved price, the schedule, and the clarified scope.
- A schedule of values the project team can use for pay applications.

## Use it on its own or with the rest

Procurement fits between [pre-construction](/services/pre-construction-services) and [construction management](/services/construction-management-services). Some owners hire PH&C for this step alone. If you have drawings and a budget, [contact us](/contact) and we will review whether the set is ready to bid.
