Utility service involves an application, a design, a deposit, and a crew that does not work for your contractor. PH&C manages that process so the building is not waiting on a connection that was never ordered.

## What PH&C handles

- Contacting water, sewer, gas, electric, and communications providers early in design.
- Submitting load information and service applications for each utility.
- Tracking each provider's design review, fees, deposits, and easement requests.
- Reconciling utility requirements with the site plan, building design, and budget.
- Coordinating utility designs with each other and with the civil engineer.
- Scheduling provider construction to fit the contractor's sequence.
- Confirming meters, inspections, and the date service will be available.

## What you get

- A utility log showing each provider, the application date, the design status, and the promised service date.
- Early identification of capacity limits, extensions, and easements.
- Costs and lead times in the budget and schedule before bidding.
- Fewer delays between a finished building and working power, water, and data.
