Residential projects run on lot counts, unit mixes, and delivery dates that builders and buyers rely on. PH&C manages residential development, from mid-size apartment buildings in urban settings to greenfield townhome and single-family communities.

## What PH&C handles

- Feasibility, zoning, and land development approvals.
- Site planning coordination, including roads, grading, utilities, and stormwater.
- Highway and traffic permits for community access.
- Environmental permits for streams, wetlands, and sewer capacity.
- Bidding and managing site contractors for earthwork, utilities, and retaining walls.
- Coordination with homebuilders on lot delivery, access, and utilities.
- Amenity areas, trails, and planting required by approvals.
- Construction management for multifamily buildings, including parking structures.

## What you get

- A land development schedule that supports the builder's start dates.
- Site costs identified and controlled before home construction begins.
- A coordinated approach to utilities, roads, and stormwater.
- One manager accountable for site delivery.
