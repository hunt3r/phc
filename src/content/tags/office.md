Office and commercial projects serve a business's daily operations, so the building has to work for the people who use it and the company that pays for it. PH&C manages the design, construction, and renovation of office and commercial buildings. We take the time to understand your core functions and work with your team through the capital program, from initial program through final occupancy.

## What PH&C handles

- Programming the space: headcount, departments, meeting areas, and growth.
- Site selection support and feasibility for new office buildings and expansions.
- Land development, utility, and building permits.
- Design coordination with architects, engineers, and interior designers.
- Estimating, value engineering, and budgeting.
- Contractor bidding and contract negotiation.
- Construction management for core and shell, interior build-out, and phased renovations.
- Coordinating move-in, technology, and furniture schedules.

## What you get

- A construction manager who acts as an extension of your staff.
- Costs and schedules that tie to your corporate or tenant requirements.
- Coordination of occupied-building work so operations continue.
- A smooth transition from construction to occupancy.
