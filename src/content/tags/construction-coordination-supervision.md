On a construction site, the superintendent works for the contractor. PH&C works for you. We are on the site to coordinate the trades, observe the work, and document conditions in a record that holds up if a question comes later.

## What PH&C handles

- Attending site meetings and issuing minutes with decisions and due dates.
- Coordinating contractors, subcontractors, designers, and inspectors.
- Observing work in progress against the contract documents.
- Keeping daily notes, photographs, and a submittal log.
- Tracking open items, punch lists, and corrective work.
- Coordinating with occupied tenants, neighbors, and adjacent operations when the site is active.
- Reporting site conditions, progress, and concerns to the owner on a regular schedule.
- Supporting closeout, including final inspections, warranties, and as-built documents.

## What you get

- A complete record of what happened on the site, and when.
- Early notice of conditions that affect cost or schedule.
- Contractors who know someone is watching on the owner's behalf.
- A single person on site who can answer your questions.
