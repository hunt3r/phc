Delivery is the date a tenant can begin its own work, and sometimes the date it can open. The lease defines both. PH&C coordinates the landlord's construction so the space a tenant receives matches what the lease promised.

## What PH&C handles

- Turning lease delivery requirements into a checklist of measurable items.
- Coordinating the landlord's contractor with the tenant's contractor, designers, and consultants.
- Sequencing base-building work, utilities, and tenant access.
- Reviewing tenant plans against the landlord's criteria and the building code.
- Managing the tenant's access, insurance, and site rules.
- Tracking delivery, rent commencement, and opening dates against the construction schedule.
- Walking the space with the tenant and recording acceptance or punch items.

## What you get

- A delivery condition both sides understand before work starts.
- Fewer disputes about what was included in the landlord's scope.
- Coordinated schedules between landlord and tenant work.
- A documented handoff that protects the lease.

## Who we work with

Landlords and developers delivering shell and pad sites. Retailers and operators building to a prototype. Owners finishing multiple tenant spaces in a single building.
