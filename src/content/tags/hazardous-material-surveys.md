Any project that touches an existing building carries the possibility of hazardous materials hidden in its walls, floors, mechanical systems, and finishes. Before renovation or demolition begins, PH\&C leads a comprehensive hazardous material survey to identify what is present, where it is, and how much it will cost to manage. Handled early, this is a straightforward due-diligence step. Discovered late - after abatement crews find surprises behind a wall - it becomes one of the most disruptive and expensive problems a project can face.

## Why the survey matters

Federal and state regulations require that regulated materials be identified and properly abated before they are disturbed. Beyond compliance, the presence and quantity of hazardous materials directly affect the demolition budget, the construction schedule, and even whether a building is worth renovating at all. Undocumented asbestos or lead can halt work, trigger regulatory penalties, and expose ownership to serious liability. A thorough survey turns these risks into a known, priced scope of work that can be planned, bid competitively, and scheduled - instead of an emergency uncovered mid-project.

## What the survey covers

PH\&C coordinates licensed and accredited inspectors to assess the materials most commonly regulated in commercial and institutional buildings, including:

* Asbestos-containing materials (ACM) - floor tile and mastic, pipe and boiler insulation, roofing, joint compound, ceiling textures, and transite.
* Lead-based paint and lead-containing coatings on walls, trim, and structural steel.
* PCBs in older light ballasts, caulking, and transformers.
* Mercury in thermostats, switches, and fluorescent lamps.
* Mold and moisture-damaged materials, where indoor air quality is a concern.
* Universal wastes and other regulated building components that require special handling and disposal.

The work typically involves a walk-through inspection, representative sampling of suspect materials, and accredited laboratory analysis. The deliverable is a survey report that documents the type, location, condition, and estimated quantity of each regulated material, along with the applicable regulatory requirements. Where a building will be demolished, we ensure the survey meets the more rigorous standard demanded by NESHAP and local demolition permitting, so nothing regulated is left unaccounted for when the structure comes down.

## How PH\&C adds value

As your project manager, PH\&C makes sure the survey is scoped to the actual scope of work - a targeted renovation survey looks very different from a pre-demolition survey, which must be exhaustive. We commission the right inspection, translate the findings into an abatement scope and budget, and fold that scope into the overall project schedule and bid documents so contractors are pricing a defined condition rather than padding for the unknown.

Just as important, we manage the downstream steps: qualifying and procuring licensed abatement contractors, coordinating required notifications and permits with the governing agencies, arranging clearance testing, and ensuring proper manifesting and disposal of regulated waste. We keep the abatement, demolition, and construction sequences aligned so the work flows without gaps or rework.

Ordering hazardous material surveys during feasibility - before design and before a purchase commitment - gives our clients the leverage to accurately budget abatement, weigh renovation against demolition, and avoid the costly, schedule-wrecking surprises that come from discovering regulated materials after construction has already started.
