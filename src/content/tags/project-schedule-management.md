A schedule that is not updated shows last month's plan. PH&C manages the construction schedule after work begins. We track what finished, what slipped, and which delay changes the completion date.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing the contractor's baseline and monthly updates.
- Tracking actual progress against planned progress.
- Identifying the critical path and the activities with float.
- Separating delays caused by the contractor, the owner, the designers, weather, and outside agencies.
- Reviewing recovery plans and requests for time extensions.
- Coordinating look-ahead schedules with trades, inspectors, and utilities.
- Reporting changes to the owner, the lender, and tenants with dates attached.

## What you get

- Plain-language schedule updates that name the activity, the cause, and the days affected.
- Early warning when a late approval or delivery threatens the opening.
- A documented basis for time-extension decisions.
- Fewer surprises at the end of the project.

## How it connects

The baseline comes from the [project schedule](/services/project-schedule) created during pre-construction. Coordination with inspectors and utilities is covered under [government agency coordination](/services/government-agency-coordination) and [utility coordination](/services/utility-coordination). Schedule management is part of [construction management](/services/construction-management-services). To review a schedule on a project in progress, [contact PH&C](/contact).
