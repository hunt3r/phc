The building permit is the municipality's review of the architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and accessibility drawings. Land development approval does not replace it. PH&C manages the building permit process so the contractor can mobilize when planned.

## What PH&C handles

- Learning the review process and submission requirements of the jurisdiction.
- Assembling the application, signed and sealed drawings, energy forms, and special inspection documents.
- Confirming that zoning and land development approvals are attached or referenced as required.
- Tracking plan review by discipline and logging each comment.
- Reviewing the design team's responses before they are submitted.
- Managing deferred submittals such as fire protection and engineered components.
- Coordinating trade permits, inspections, and the certificate of occupancy.

## What you get

- A permit date on the project schedule, with realistic review time included.
- Comments answered once and completely, which avoids repeated review cycles.
- Early notice when review is running long, while you can still adjust mobilization.
- A permit file organized for inspectors, lenders, and closeout.
