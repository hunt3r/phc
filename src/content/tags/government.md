PH&C has been verified by CVE as a Service Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). We support public-sector clients with project management and oversight on federal, state, and local projects.

PH&C works with the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) as a consultant to Booz Allen Hamilton. The role is project management oversight on FTA projects around the country. The assignment includes checking that government grant funds, including American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) funds, are used for their grant purposes and in accordance with federal regulations and standard industry practice. PH&C provides the person on the ground and the expertise on transit projects.

## What PH&C handles

- Project management oversight and document review for grant-funded projects.
- Field review of cost, schedule, design, construction, and quality records.
- Procurement and bidding that follow public-sector rules.
- Coordination with municipalities, authorities, and state agencies.
- Budget and schedule control on publicly funded construction.
- Records and reporting for grant administrators and auditors.

## What you get

- An experienced team that understands public procurement and grant compliance.
- Independent reporting on cost, schedule, and quality.
- Documentation that stands up to review.
