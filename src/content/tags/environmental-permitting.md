Some environmental approvals sit outside the stormwater permit. Stream and wetland crossings, and sewage facilities planning modules, are reviewed by county and state agencies and often have to be in place before the municipality will sign a plan. PH&C coordinates those approvals with your civil engineer and the agencies.

## What PH&C handles

- Identifying streams, wetlands, floodplains, and other regulated features from mapping and field delineation.
- Coordinating permit applications for crossings and encroachments, including construction method and restoration details.
- Managing sewage facilities planning, including flow estimates and the municipal and state review.
- Aligning each environmental submission with the land development plan.
- Tracking agency comments and resubmissions.
- Building the review periods into the project schedule and the construction sequence.
- Coordinating required inspections and closeout.

## What you get

- A list of the environmental permits your site needs, with the agency and review time for each.
- Submissions that match the plans the township is reviewing.
- Early warning when a crossing or a sewer capacity issue could change the layout.
- A schedule you can plan financing and construction around.
