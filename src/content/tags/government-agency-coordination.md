During construction, the agencies come back. The building inspector, the fire marshal, the township engineer, the conservation district, and the highway authority each have a list. PH&C puts their visits on the schedule and gets their comments closed.

## What PH&C handles

- Building a list of required inspections by trade and by site feature.
- Scheduling each inspection for when the work is visible and ready.
- Coordinating building, fire, health, stormwater, highway, and utility inspectors.
- Attending the inspections that affect the certificate of occupancy or the release of security.
- Recording comments, assigning corrective work, and tracking reinspection.
- Keeping approved plans, permit cards, and inspection records on file.
- Coordinating temporary and final certificates of occupancy.
- Reporting agency requirements that change cost or schedule.

## What you get

- Fewer failed inspections caused by timing or notice.
- A written record of every agency approval.
- Early warning of conditions that could hold up occupancy.
- A smoother path from final construction to opening.

## How it connects

The permits that precede this work are managed under [building and construction permits](/services/building-construction-permits). Release of municipal security is handled under [escrow and letter-of-credit reductions](/services/escrow-letter-of-credit-reductions).
