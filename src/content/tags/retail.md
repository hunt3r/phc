Retail projects are built to a date. A retailer, a landlord, a lender, and a township each have a stake in it. PH&C manages retail development from site selection through opening for owners, developers, REITs, and retailers.

PH&C has worked with some of the region's most demanding retailers, including [AT&T](https://www.att.com/), [Burlington Coat Factory](https://www.burlington.com/), [Chick-fil-A](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/), [CVS](https://www.cvs.com/), [Five Guys](https://www.fiveguys.com/), [Giant](https://giantfoodstores.com/), [Jersey Mike's](https://www.jerseymikes.com/), [Lidl US](https://www.lidl.com/), [Mattress Warehouse](https://sleephappens.com/), [Panda Express](https://www.pandaexpress.com/), [PNC Bank](https://www.pnc.com/), [Ross](https://www.rossstores.com/), [Safeway](https://www.safeway.com/), [ShopRite](https://shop.shoprite.com/store/d483123802), [Staples](https://www.staples.com/), [Target](https://www.target.com/), [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/), [Walgreens](https://www.walgreens.com/), [Wawa](https://www.wawa.com/), and [Wegmans](https://www.wegmans.com/).

## What PH&C handles

- Site analysis and feasibility for new pads and redevelopment sites.
- Land development, highway, stormwater, and utility approvals.
- Demolition and remediation of former commercial and fuel sites.
- Delivering pad-ready sites to a retailer's specifications.
- Building and tenant construction coordination with retailers and their contractors.
- Shopping center repositioning, including facade renovation, interior renovation, and re-tenanting.
- Construction of new buildings for national and regional brands.

## What you get

- One manager coordinating the landlord, the tenant, the design team, and the contractors.
- A schedule built around the retailer's required opening date.
- Site costs and approvals identified before the lease is signed.
- A smooth handoff to the retailer at delivery.
