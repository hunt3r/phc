If a driveway touches a state road, the building permit does not govern the opening. PH&C manages the highway occupancy permit with PennDOT, the access permit with NJDOT, and the local road permits when the frontage is a county or municipal street.

## What PH&C handles

- Identifying which agency controls each road frontage and which permits apply.
- Coordinating the traffic engineer and civil engineer on the traffic study, access design, and highway plans.
- Preparing and submitting permit applications and responding to agency comments.
- Aligning the highway plans with the land development plan, so both approvals describe the same driveway.
- Bidding and managing off-site improvements such as turn lanes, signals, and drainage work.
- Scheduling agency inspections during construction.
- Closing out permits and any security posted for the work.

## What you get

- A permit path with the agencies, submissions, and review times laid out.
- Early pricing of required road improvements, which belong in the budget from the start.
- Highway, municipal, and building documents that agree with each other.
- Fewer delays between completed construction and an approved opening.
