Municipal approval is often the longest and least predictable part of a project. PH&C manages it for you. We guide the design team through the township's subdivision and land development requirements and pursue any variance, conditional use, or rezoning the project needs.

## What PH&C handles

- Reading the ordinance and choosing the approval path before the plan is drawn.
- Preparing and coordinating the submission package: site plans, stormwater, traffic, and application forms.
- Tracking staff, planning commission, and outside-agency review comments by department.
- Preparing the design team and the owner for public meetings and hearings.
- Responding to comment letters with a single, coordinated set of revisions.
- Converting conditions of approval into design changes, budget items, and bid requirements.
- Keeping the approved plan consistent with highway, stormwater, utility, and building permits.

## What you get

- A clear map of the approvals your project needs and how long each is likely to take.
- A manager who watches deadlines, meeting schedules, and review periods.
- Fewer rounds of comments, because responses are checked before they are filed.
- Early notice of conditions that add cost.
