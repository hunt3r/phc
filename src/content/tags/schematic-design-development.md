Schematic design shows the building on the site. Design development decides the systems, materials, and details the estimate will rely on. PH\&C guides the architect and engineers through both phases and reviews the documents on your behalf at each milestone.

## What PH\&C handles

* Reviewing drawings against the owner's program, budget, and schedule.
* Checking the plan against zoning, parking, and site requirements.
* Coordinating the architectural, civil, structural, and mechanical, electrical, plumbing (MEP) disciplines.
* Pricing each milestone set and comparing it with the previous estimate.
* Tracking open items and decisions that belong to the owner.

## What you get

* Drawings that match the program and the budget before they move to the next phase.
* Conflicts between disciplines found on paper instead of in the field.
* Clear choices when a design grows beyond the budget, with price options presented.
* A cleaner set for permitting and bidding, which means fewer addenda and fewer change orders.

## How it connects

The program that drives the design comes from [initial program development](/services/initial-program-development). Specialty systems are handled through [special construction design coordination](/services/special-construction-design-coordination). Pricing runs through [estimating and value engineering](/services/project-estimating-value-engineering).
