A bid is an offer. The contract is the agreement. PH&C negotiates it for the owner so the approved price, the clarified scope, and the committed schedule are what the parties sign.

## What PH&C handles

- Selecting a contract form the owner can administer, or working from the owner's own form.
- Reviewing general conditions, insurance, bonding, retainage, and payment terms.
- Attaching a scope exhibit that states what is included and what is excluded.
- Establishing a schedule of values and a milestone schedule.
- Setting change-order procedures, pricing rules, and markups.
- Addressing delay provisions, liquidated damages, or incentives where the project calls for them.
- Aligning the contract with lender, grant, and tenant requirements.
- Coordinating review by the owner's attorney.

## What you get

- An agreement that matches the deal reached during bidding.
- Clear responsibility for permits, utilities, testing, and site conditions.
- Terms that protect your financing and your tenant commitments.
- A contract that the project team can administer from the first pay application.
