PH&C supports federal clients with project management oversight on grant-funded construction. The role is independent: we review cost, schedule, design, construction, and quality records, and we report what we find.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing project documents, including contracts, budgets, schedules, and design submissions.
- Visiting project sites to compare the work in place with the reports submitted.
- Checking that grant funds are used for the purpose in the grant and in line with federal regulations.
- Reviewing change orders, payment requests, and contingency use.
- Evaluating risk and recommending corrective action.
- Preparing written reports for the oversight agency.
- Coordinating with grantees, designers, and contractors.

## Why federal clients use PH&C

- Our team understands both construction practice and the compliance side of public funding.
- We provide a person on the ground, not only a review of paperwork.
- PH&C is a verified Service Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB).

## What you get

- Clear findings that separate documented facts from concerns.
- Reports organized around the oversight criteria the agency uses.
- Practical recommendations that grantees can act on.
