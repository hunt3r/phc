The bid opening is not the selection. PH&C takes the proposals apart, asks the questions, and tells you which contractor priced the work you intend to build.

## What PH&C handles

- Building a leveling sheet with base bid, alternates, unit prices, and allowances in the same rows for each bidder.
- Identifying exclusions, qualifications, and assumptions that change what a number includes.
- Sending written clarification questions and recording the answers.
- Checking schedules, staffing, insurance, bonding, and references.
- Interviewing finalists when the project warrants it.
- Following the procurement rules that apply to public and grant-funded work.
- Presenting a recommendation with the reasons behind it.

## What you get

- A clear comparison of what each bid includes.
- A cleared number, with the remaining exclusions listed and priced.
- A recommendation that considers price, schedule, experience, and the team assigned to your project.
- A file that documents how the selection was made.

## How it connects

The invitation that bidders answer is the [RFP](/services/rfp-development). The agreement that follows is developed under [contractor negotiations and contracts](/services/contractor-negotiations-contracts). Both are part of [contractor procurement](/services/project-contractor-procurement). If you have bids in hand and need help comparing them, [contact PH&C](/contact).
