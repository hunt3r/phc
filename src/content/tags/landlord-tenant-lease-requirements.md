A lease is a construction document written by lawyers. PH&C reads the work letter, the delivery exhibit, and the clauses on allowances and delays, then turns them into a list the project team can build from: landlord scope, tenant scope, delivery condition, date, and cost.

## What PH&C handles

- Reviewing lease exhibits, work letters, and tenant design criteria.
- Defining terms such as pad ready, warm shell, and turnkey in specific, measurable items.
- Listing the landlord's and the tenant's work, and the responsibility for permits, utilities, and fees.
- Reviewing delivery dates, delay provisions, and remedies against the realistic schedule.
- Comparing the retailer's or tenant's prototype requirements with the site and the budget.
- Identifying allowances, reimbursements, and who owns the plans.
- Flagging requirements the landlord cannot meet before the lease is signed.

## What you get

- A construction matrix everyone can initial.
- Fewer arguments at delivery, because criteria are measurable.
- Cost and schedule effects of lease terms before signature.
- A clear handoff into [tenant delivery coordination](/services/tenant-delivery-coordination).
