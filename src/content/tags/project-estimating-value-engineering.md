An estimate lists the work and puts a price on each line. Value engineering is what you do when the total comes in above the budget. PH\&C has the ability to identify value engineering opportunities to explore if requested.

## What PH\&C handles

* Quantity-based estimates that follow the drawings, with site work, building, and tenant improvements priced separately.
* Allowances and unit prices for conditions that are not yet known, such as rock, unsuitable soils, and utility conflicts.
* Contingency identified by risk instead of buried in a single percentage.
* Independent review of the architect's and contractor's pricing.
* Value-engineering options priced line by line, with the effect on schedule, quality, and operating cost noted.
* Re-pricing at each design milestone.

## What you get

* A clear picture of what drives the total and which items are firm.
* Alternatives you can accept or decline, each with a dollar amount and a stated trade-off.
* An estimate that matches the bid form, which makes bids easier to compare later.
* Fewer cost surprises during procurement and construction.
