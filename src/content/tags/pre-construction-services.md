Pre-construction is where an owner's budget and schedule are won or lost. PH&C manages that phase on your behalf. We assemble the design team, track the approvals, test the cost against your pro forma, and bring the project to bid with a scope contractors can price.

## What PH&C handles

- Program development that turns your goals into uses, square footage, quality level, budget, and target dates.
- Design coordination through schematic design and design development, with the architect and engineers working from one set of requirements.
- Land development, zoning, highway, stormwater, environmental, utility, and building approvals, scheduled against the critical path.
- Detailed estimating and value engineering while the drawings can still change.
- Contractor procurement, including bid documents, bid leveling, and contract negotiation.
- Pre-construction meetings that put the owner, the designers, and the contractors on the same plan before work begins.

## What you get

- One manager responsible for the plan, the budget, and the schedule.
- Cost feedback at each design milestone, so decisions are made with prices attached.
- An approval calendar that names each agency, each submission, and each dependency.
- A bid package that contractors can price on equal terms.
- A clean handoff into construction management, with the baseline schedule and budget already in place.
