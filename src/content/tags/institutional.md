Institutions build to serve a mission, and their projects often have to work around daily operations. PH&C assists institutional clients with design, value engineering, and construction management for the projects a campus requires. We take the time to understand your core functions and work with your team through the capital program, from initial program through final dedication.

## What PH&C handles

- Program development with administrators, faculty, and facility staff.
- Master planning support and feasibility for new buildings, additions, and site improvements.
- Land development, highway, and stormwater approvals.
- Design coordination and value engineering.
- Public and private procurement, including competitive bidding.
- Construction management with phasing around classes, services, and events.
- Site work such as access roads, trails, parking, and utilities.
- Closeout, training, and warranty coordination.

## What you get

- A construction manager who acts as an extension of your staff.
- Budgets and schedules aligned with academic calendars and board approvals.
- Procurement that follows the rules governing your organization.
- A completed project that supports your mission.
