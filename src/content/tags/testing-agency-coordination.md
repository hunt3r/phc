Materials testing and special inspections show whether the soils, concrete, and steel match the drawings. PH&C coordinates the testing agency, puts inspections on the schedule, and reads the reports for the owner.

## What PH&C handles

- Identifying the tests and special inspections the building code and the design team require.
- Coordinating the selection and scope of the testing agency.
- Scheduling inspections to match the contractor's pours, fills, and erections.
- Reviewing results for soils compaction, concrete strength, reinforcing, structural steel, and fireproofing.
- Reporting failed or incomplete tests to the owner and the engineer immediately.
- Tracking retests and corrective work until the item is closed.
- Assembling the testing record for the building department, the lender, and closeout.

## What you get

- Testing that happens before the work is covered.
- Reports that reach the people who can act on them.
- A clear record that supports the certificate of occupancy and final payment.
- Early warning when the field does not match the design.
