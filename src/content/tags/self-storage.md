Demand for multi-story, climate-controlled self-storage has changed the market. With several systems and product types available, PH&C guides owners through the design and construction options and recommends an economical building that fits the project's needs, constraints, and market.

## What PH&C handles

- Feasibility and site analysis, including zoning and access.
- Land development approvals and stormwater design for large roof and paving areas.
- Utility service for climate control, fire protection, and technology.
- Comparing structural systems, such as light-gauge framing, steel, and masonry, for cost and schedule.
- Coordinating exterior materials and roof systems.
- Coordinating the door, corridor, and unit-system contractors with the base building.
- Construction management, inspections, and certificate of occupancy.
- Closeout, including the operator's turnover requirements.

## What you get

- A cost comparison of the structural and exterior options.
- A schedule that accounts for the long lead times on doors, partitions, and equipment.
- A managed relationship with the specialty contractors who deliver the rentable space.
- A finished facility that is ready for the operator.
