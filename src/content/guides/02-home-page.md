The homepage is built from two places: the **Home** item in the left menu, and the projects, quotes, and videos you mark elsewhere.

## The banner at the top

If at least one project is marked **Featured** and has a cover image, the top of the homepage is a slideshow of those projects. It shows up to five, using each project's title, short description, and cover image. Each slide links to that project.

The slideshow uses three settings from **Home**, under **Hero**:

- **Size** is **full** (a tall banner) or **compact** (a shorter one).
- **Overlay Opacity** is how dark or light the tint is, from 0 to 100. A higher number makes the photo dimmer so the title is easier to read.
- **Overlay Color** is **dark** or **light**.

If no project is both featured and has a cover image, the slideshow is replaced by a text banner. That banner uses the rest of the **Hero** fields:

- **Eyebrow** is a small line above the main headline.
- **Tagline** is the headline.
- **Subtitle** is the sentence under the headline.
- **Primary CTA Text** and **Primary CTA Link** are the main button. The link can be a page on this site, such as `/contact`, or a full web address.
- **Secondary CTA Text** and **Secondary CTA Link** are the second button.
- **Background Image** is the photo behind the text.

Size, overlay opacity, and overlay color apply to this banner too.

## What appears under the banner

**Content Cards** on **Home** are promotional blocks placed directly under the banner, before the project list. See **Photos, videos, and content cards** for how to build one.

**Latest projects** is a grid of project cards. Projects are ordered by the **Order** number on each project, smallest first. A project with no Order is placed last. **Max Latest Projects** sets how many cards to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 3.

**Videos** on the homepage are videos you marked **Promote to home page**, from any project, tag, About, Contact, or other page. **Max Videos** sets how many to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 8. Every video, featured or not, is also listed on the Videos page.

**What our clients say** uses quotes you marked **Promote to home page** on a project. **Max Testimonials** sets how many to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 8. Every quote, featured or not, is also listed on the Testimonials page.

## Page Title

**Page Title** on **Home** is the name shown in the browser tab and in search results for the homepage. It does not appear as a heading on the page itself. The sentence search engines use for the homepage is the Hero **Subtitle**. See **Search engines and AI**.
