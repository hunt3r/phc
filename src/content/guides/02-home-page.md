The homepage is built from **Home** in the left menu, plus the projects, tags, quotes, and videos you mark elsewhere.

## The banner at the top

The top of the homepage is a slideshow. It mixes three kinds of slides:

- A project marked **Featured** that has a cover image, when **Show featured projects** is on. The slide uses that project's title, short description, and cover image, and links to the project. The button says View project.
- A tag marked **Featured** that has an image, when **Show featured tags** is on. The slide uses that tag's label, description, and image, and links to the tag's page. The button says View, followed by the tag's label. See **Sectors, services, and tags**.
- A custom slide you add on **Home**, under **Hero**, in **Slides**.

**Order**, under **Hero**, is the sequence of the three groups. Drag a row to move that group. The default is **Slides**, then **Tags**, then **Projects**. A group that is missing from the list is added at the end in that same default order.

Inside a group, **Hero order** sets the sequence. A smaller number comes first. Leave **Hero order** blank on a custom slide to keep the order of the **Slides** list. Leave it blank on a project or a tag to place that item after the numbered ones in its group, then by name. **Hero order** on a project does not change that project's **Order** in the portfolio or in Latest projects. **Hero order** on a tag does not change that tag's **Order** in the menus.

**Max Hero Slides**, under **Hero**, sets how many slides to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 5. Slides past that count are left off the end of this order.

A project or tag with **Featured** turned on but no image is left out of the slideshow.

**Show featured projects** and **Show featured tags**, under **Hero**, turn those groups on or off. Both start on. Turn one off to hide that group without changing **Featured** on each project or tag.

The slideshow uses three settings from **Home**, under **Hero**. They apply to every slide, including custom ones:

- **Size** is **full** (a tall banner) or **compact** (a shorter one).
- **Overlay Opacity** is how dark or light the tint is, from 0 to 100. A higher number makes the photo dimmer so the title is easier to read.
- **Overlay Color** is **dark** or **light**.

### Custom slides

Add a slide under **Slides**. Each one has a **Design**:

- **Slide** matches a project slide: an image, a **Title**, a **Description**, and one button. **Button text** and **Button link** are that button. The link can be a page on this site, such as `/contact`, or a full web address. If you set a link and leave the button text blank, the button says View, followed by the title. A Slide with no image is left out.
- **Banner** matches the text banner: **Eyebrow** is a small line above the headline, **Title** is the headline, and **Description** is the sentence under it. **Button text** and **Button link** are the main button. **Second button text** and **Second button link** are the second button. **Image** is the photo behind the text. A Banner can appear without a photo. Fields that belong to the other design are ignored.

**Hero order** on the slide places it among the other custom slides. Leave it blank to keep the order of this list.

If no project, tag, or custom slide qualifies, the homepage has no banner. A **Banner** slide is how you add a text banner to the slideshow.

## What appears under the banner

**Content Cards** on **Home** are promotional blocks placed directly under the banner, before the project list. See **Photos, videos, and content cards** for how to build one.

**Latest projects** is a grid of project cards. Projects are ordered by the **Order** number on each project, smallest first. A project with no Order is placed last. **Max Latest Projects** sets how many cards to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 3.

**Videos** on the homepage are videos you marked **Promote to home page**, from any project, tag, About, Contact, or other page. **Max Videos** sets how many to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 8. Every video, featured or not, is also listed on the Videos page.

**What our clients say** uses quotes you marked **Promote to home page** on a project. **Max Testimonials** sets how many to show. If you leave it blank, the site shows 8. Every quote, featured or not, is also listed on the Testimonials page.

## Page Title

**Page Title** on **Home** is the name shown in the browser tab and in search results for the homepage. It does not appear as a heading on the page itself. **Description** is the sentence search engines use. See **Search engines and AI**.
