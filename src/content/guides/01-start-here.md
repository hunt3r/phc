This guide is kept up to date with the website. It opens for reading. Use **Edit**, then **Save**, to correct the article. If something no longer matches the site, ask the person who manages the website.

## Signing in

Open the website and add `/admin` to the end of the address, then sign in with the account you were given. After you sign in, the left menu lists every part of the site you can change.

## What the left menu is

Each item in the left menu is a section of the website:

- **How to use this site** is this guide. An article opens for reading. Click **Edit** to change it, then **Save** to update it. Names of the other articles are links, so you can open one without going back to the list. Start with this article, then open the others as you need them. **How the website is put together** explains the services behind the site and what happens after you save.
- **Portfolio** is the project pages.
- **Entities** are the companies, people, and organizations named on a project, such as the client, architect, engineer, or contractor. See **Portfolio projects**.
- **Tags** are the sectors and services. They power the Portfolio menu, the Services page, and the checkboxes on each project.
- **About Us** is the About page.
- **Contact** is the Contact page, including the address and email that also appear in the footer.
- **Pages** are extra pages such as the privacy policy. Each one gets its own address on the site.
- **Staff** is the people shown on the About page. Each person also gets a profile page.
- **Site Settings** is the header menu, the footer menu, and the short description under the logo in the footer.
- **Home** is the homepage: how many projects, quotes, and videos to show, and the backup banner used when no project is featured.

## Making a change

1. Click the section in the left menu.
2. Click the item you want to change. A single item, such as Home or About Us, opens straight into the form.
3. Edit the fields. The names on the form are the same names used in the rest of this guide.
4. Click **Save**.

Saving publishes the change. The live website updates after a short rebuild, usually within a minute or two. Refresh the public page if you do not see it yet.

Many sections also open the live page while you edit, so you can click the part of the page you want to change. **Staff** and **Site Settings** open the form instead.

## If you are not sure where something lives

- A project: open **Portfolio**.
- A client, architect, engineer, or contractor: open **Entities** to change the name, website, or logo. To put one on a project, open the project in **Portfolio**. See **Portfolio projects**.
- Which projects appear in the top slideshow: open the project in **Portfolio** and turn on **Featured**. The project also needs a cover image.
- A sector or a service page: open **Tags**.
- The About writeup: open **About Us**.
- A person on the team: open **Staff**.
- The address, phone, or email: open **Contact**.
- The menus or the footer blurb: open **Site Settings**.
- The privacy policy, or a new simple page: open **Pages**.
- How many projects, quotes, or videos appear on the homepage: open **Home**.
- A photo, a video, or a promotional card: read **Photos, videos, and content cards**.
- How a page shows up in search, or what AI tools read: read **Search engines and AI**.
- Which accounts the site uses, or what happens after **Save**: read **How the website is put together**.
