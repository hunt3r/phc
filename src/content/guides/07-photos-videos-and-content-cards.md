Photos, videos, and content cards show up on many sections. They work the same way wherever you see them.

## Photos

Image fields say to upload through the media library. Click the field, choose an existing photo or upload a new one, and select it. You do not paste a file path.

Use a clear photo and fill in **Alt text** when that field is offered. Alt text is a short description of the image for people who cannot see it, and it helps when the image fails to load.

Where images show up:

- A project's **Hero / cover image** is the main photo, the card image, and the homepage slide image.
- A project's **Gallery** is the thumbnail grid lower on the project page.
- A tag's **Image** is used on that sector or service page.
- **Featured Image** on About, Contact, and Pages is the banner at the top.
- A **Profile Photo** on a staff member is optional.

## Videos

A video field asks for a **YouTube Video URL**. Paste the full YouTube link, or the 11-character video id. The site builds the player from that.

- **Title** is the name on the video card.
- **Description** is the short text with the video.
- **Thumbnail Override** is optional. Upload a photo to replace the picture YouTube provides.
- **Promote to home page** also places the video in the homepage Videos section, up to the **Max Videos** number on **Home**.

A video always appears on the page where you added it: a project, a tag, About, Contact, or another page. Every video from all of those places is also collected on the Videos page, whether or not it is promoted.

Drag videos to reorder them. The homepage uses promoted videos from across the site, not a separate homepage list.

## Content cards

**Content Cards** are promotional blocks: a heading, some text, an optional image, and an optional button. They appear on Home, projects, tags, About, Contact, and Pages.

On **Home**, cards sit under the banner and above Latest projects. On other pages, they sit under the written body and above any project list.

Each card has:

- **Title**, shown as a heading. Optional.
- **Content**, the rich text inside the card. You can use headings, bold, lists, and links.
- **Image**, optional.
- **CTA Text** and **CTA Link**, the button label and where it goes. Either can be left blank.
- **Layout**: **vertical** stacks the image above the text. **horizontal** places them side by side.
- **Width**: **contained** stays within the page width. **full** stretches to the edges of the window. On a page that already has a side menu, a full card fills that column instead of the whole window.
- **Background Color**: **none**, **primary**, **secondary**, **brand-blue**, **surface**, or **brand-beige**. Use a color with **full** width when you want a colored band.
- **Text Size**: **sm**, **base**, **lg**, or **xl**.
- **Image Position** applies only to a horizontal layout: **left** or **right**.
- **Match image height to content** applies only to a horizontal layout. It crops the image to the height of the text, from the center.
- **Image Gallery** is an optional row of thumbnails under the card text. Each image can have alt text, a link, and a button label. The button label defaults to "View project".

Drag cards to reorder them on the page.
