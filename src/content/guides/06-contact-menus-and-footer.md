This guide is kept up to date with the website. Please read it here, and don't edit it. If something no longer matches the site, ask the person who manages the website.

## Contact

**Contact** in the left menu is the Contact page. There is only one, and it cannot be deleted.

- **Title** is the page heading.
- **Description** is the line under the heading on the banner, and the preview text when the page is shared.
- **Featured Image** is the photo behind that banner.
- **Contact Info (footer)** is the address block. It appears in the footer on every page and in the contact card on the Contact page. You can use bold text and links, including an email link such as `info@phandc.net`.
- **Videos** and **Content Cards** appear on the Contact page. See **Photos, videos, and content cards**.
- **Body** is the message above the form, such as how you'd like people to get in touch.

The form itself (name, email, phone, company, and the rest) is part of the website. Those fields are not edited in this admin. Messages sent through the form are delivered by the site's form handler, not stored in this menu.

## Site Settings

**Site Settings** holds the menus and the footer blurb. There is only one settings entry, and it cannot be deleted. It opens as a form.

- **Footer Description** is the short paragraph under the logo in the footer.
- **Header Links** are the links in the top menu. **Label** is the words visitors see. **URL** is the destination: a path such as `/about`, or a full web address. Turn on **Open in new tab** when the link should leave the site in a new tab.
- **Footer Links** work the same way, for the menu in the footer.

Drag a link to reorder it. Use the list controls to add or remove one. A new **Pages** entry does not appear in either menu until you add a link for it here.

The logo, the dark-mode button, and the social icons are part of the website. They are not edited in Site Settings.
