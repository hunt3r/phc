Lidl asked PH\&C to manage construction of its new store on Route 303 in Tappan, New York. NORR was the architect, Bohler was the civil engineer and B. Tait Builders was the contractor.

PH\&C served as Lidl's construction manager on the project, representing the owner's interests throughout construction.

Key tasks and functions included:

* Ran weekly progress meetings and issued meeting minutes
* Tracked the milestone schedule and reported progress to Lidl
* Reviewed the general contractor's payment applications and change orders
* Coordinated Lidl's vendors with the construction schedule
* Coordinated third-party testing and inspections
* Managed commissioning and the punch list
* Managed closeout documents and the handover to Lidl

We're working hard on many more new store projects, so stay tuned for the next grand opening!
