Lidl asked PH\&C to manage construction of its new store on Route 303 in Tappan, New York. NORR was the architect, Bohler was the civil engineer and B. Tait Builders was the contractor.

As Lidl's External Construction Manager, PH\&C served as the Owner's Representative from GC kickoff through commissioning and turnover. The project was a new 25,650 SF single-story Lidl prototype store with an integrated bakery, loading dock and full site work coordinated with the adjacent shopping center.

Key tasks and functions included:

* Lead weekly owner-architect-contractor meetings with Lidl, the design team and the general contractor, and issue meeting minutes.
* Manage the milestone schedule from site mobilization through store turnover, communicating schedule updates to Lidl weekly.
* Review GC milestone payment applications and change orders and present them to Lidl for approval.
* Coordinate Lidl's refrigeration, signage, IT and security vendors with the general contractor.
* Manage third-party testing and inspections and coordinate building department inspections for occupancy.
* Coordinate store commissioning (FPT and BMS-FPT) and manage the consolidated punch list to completion.
* Complete project closeout and hand the store over to Lidl's Facilities and Sales teams.

We're working hard on many more new store projects, so stay tuned for the next grand opening!
